A few things you might wonder

Before we talk.

I’m not sure what I need yet.

That’s a good place to start. Bring the problem: too many missed enquiries, a website that feels out of date, or a task that takes too long. We can figure out a sensible first step.

Do I need AI in my business?

Only if it helps with a real task. A clear website or a simple form may be the better answer. For an AI feature, we’ll decide what it should do, how to check its output, and when a person should step in.

What will it cost?

The price depends on the scope, integrations, and content involved. After a conversation, I’ll put together a proposal with the deliverables, timeline, and any ongoing tool costs before work begins.
