Rise

An AI-powered SEO overhaul that grew organic traffic by 180% in 4 months for an e-commerce brand.

ClientBazaar Direct
Timeline8 weeks (ongoing)
RoleTechnical SEO + content strategy
StackPython · GA4 · SEMrush · AI Writers

The Challenge

Bazaar Direct had 12,000+ product pages but only 8,000 monthly organic visitors. Their site was technically sound but had thin content, poor internal linking, and zero content strategy beyond product descriptions.

They were being outranked by competitors with worse products but better SEO. They needed a systematic approach — not one-off fixes.

Our Solution

We deployed Rise — an AI-driven SEO growth engine:

  • Technical audit + fix of 140+ issues (core web vitals, schema markup, crawl optimization)
  • AI-generated category guides and buying guides (200+ pages of high-quality content)
  • Intelligent internal linking overhaul — connected 12K pages through topical clusters
  • Weekly content calendar driven by real-time keyword gap analysis
