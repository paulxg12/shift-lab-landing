✦ CASE STUDY

Synapse

A custom NLP pipeline that automated contract review for a legal tech company — 90% accuracy, 60% faster turnaround.

ClientLexAI (LegalTech)
Timeline14 weeks
RoleEnd-to-end custom build
StackPython · spaCy · Transformers · FastAPI

The Challenge

LexAI had a team of 15 paralegals manually reviewing 500+ contracts a week. Each contract took 2-3 hours. Burnout was high, errors slipped through, and they couldn't scale to the demand their sales team was generating.

Off-the-shelf solutions didn't work — legal language is too domain-specific, and their clients required clause-level accuracy that generic models couldn't deliver.

Our Solution

We built Synapse — a completely custom NLP pipeline trained on their contract corpus:

  • Fine-tuned transformer model on 10K+ labeled legal documents (NDAs, MSAs, SOWs)
  • Clause classification — identified 40+ clause types with 94% precision
  • Risk scoring — flagged high-risk clauses (indemnification, liability caps, auto-renewal)
  • Interactive review UI — paralegals could approve, reject, or edit AI suggestions in one click

Tech Stack

Python spaCy Hugging Face FastAPI React PostgreSQL Docker AWS ECS

The Results

90%Overall accuracy
60%Faster review time
94%Clause precision
500+Contracts/week processed
