Vertex

A high-converting product site that drove $2M+ in pipeline within 90 days of launch.

ClientLayer SaaS
Timeline4 weeks
RoleDesign + development
StackNext.js · Framer Motion · Vercel

The Challenge

Layer had a great B2B product but an outdated website that looked like it was built in 2019. Their conversion rate was stuck at 0.8%, demo requests were flat, and their bounce rate was over 70%.

They needed a complete redesign — not just pretty, but engineered to convert. Messaging, layout, performance, and SEO all had to work together.

Our Solution

We rebuilt Vertex from scratch with conversion as the north star:

  • Performance-first architecture — sub-1s load times, 98 Lighthouse score
  • Strategic information architecture designed to guide visitors toward the demo CTA
  • Interactive product demos embedded directly in the page (no video, real interactions)
  • Case study section with measurable outcomes — social proof where it matters most

Tech Stack

Next.js Framer Motion Three.js Sanity CMS Vercel Google Analytics 4

The Results

3.5%Conversion rate (was 0.8%)
$2M+Pipeline in 90 days
98Lighthouse score
55%Bounce rate reduction

What the client said

Our old site felt like a brochure. Vertex feels like our best salesperson — working 24/7. Demo requests tripled in the first month.

— David Kim, CEO, Layer SaaS
